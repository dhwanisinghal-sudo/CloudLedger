CloudLedger

A cloud bill turned into a cost sheet: classify, apportion, cost per user, break-even price, budget alert. Edit any input and every table updates.

Part 1: Classify the bill

Columns: service, amount (₹), product tag, driver. A line with a product tag is a direct cost. A line with no tag is indirect (shared), and its driver (users, compute or storage) decides how it is apportioned.

Part 2: Apportion shared costs

Product share = shared cost × (product's driver units ÷ total driver units). Change the driver units below.

Part 3: Cost per user, break-even and budget

Variable cost per user, selling price and budget are inputs. Fixed cost = total cost − (variable cost per user × users).
